# OctoAcme Project Management Processes

## Overview

OctoAcme delivers projects iteratively through initiation, planning, execution, release, and retrospective. Initiation validates the business need, measurable outcomes, stakeholders, risks, and resources. Planning turns approved work into a prioritized, estimated backlog with acceptance criteria, a Definition of Done, dependencies, and milestones. During execution, teams deliver small increments, track work and blockers on a project board, and review progress at sprint or milestone ends.

Releases require completed acceptance criteria, passing CI and security scans, release notes, a rollback or mitigation plan, and prepared smoke tests. Teams verify deployments after release, then use retrospectives to capture what went well, identify improvements, and assign follow-up actions. Risk management and clear ownership support delivery throughout the lifecycle.

## Roles and communication

The Project Manager coordinates schedules, delivery, risks, and communication; the Product Manager defines outcomes, prioritizes the backlog, and measures success. Developers build and test features, QA validates quality and acceptance criteria, and stakeholders provide input and approvals. Teams coordinate through regular standups, weekly delivery or PM–Product Manager syncs, monthly stakeholder updates, and sprint or milestone demos. The project board and a single source of truth keep status visible, while blockers escalate from the team through the PM and Product Lead to the sponsor as needed.

## Quality assurance

Quality is integrated into delivery with unit tests for new logic, integration tests where applicable, automated tests and linting in CI, end-to-end smoke tests for critical flows, security scanning, and manual QA when needed. Post-deployment verification and retrospective action tracking help teams address issues and continuously improve.
