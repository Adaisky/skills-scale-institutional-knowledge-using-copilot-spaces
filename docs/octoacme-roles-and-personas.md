# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Engineering / Technical Leads

### Role Summary
Engineering / Technical Leads guide technical implementation and help the delivery team make sound, maintainable engineering decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Guide architecture and technical design decisions
- Coordinate technical dependencies and resolve engineering blockers
- Surface technical risks, trade-offs, and estimates early
- Support Developers with implementation standards and code review

### Working Relationships
- Partner with the Product Manager (PdM) to assess feasibility and clarify technical implications of product trade-offs
- Keep the Project Manager (PM) informed about technical dependencies, risks, and schedule impacts
- Coordinate implementation with Developers and quality expectations with QA/Testing

### Typical Communication
- Technical design documents and architecture decision records
- Engineering planning, code reviews, and technical risk updates

---

## UX / Research Leads

### Role Summary
UX / Research Leads help ensure that solutions are usable, accessible, and grounded in customer needs.

### Responsibilities
- Plan and conduct user research or usability validation
- Turn research findings into actionable design recommendations
- Define interaction and accessibility expectations for the experience
- Share evidence and unresolved user needs with the delivery team

### Working Relationships
- Partner with the PdM to connect user needs and research findings to product goals and priorities
- Work with Developers and QA/Testing to validate usability and accessibility in the delivered experience
- Involve Stakeholders in research findings and design decisions where their input is needed
- Coordinate research timing and dependencies with the PM

### Typical Communication
- Research plans, findings, and design artifacts
- Design reviews and usability or accessibility feedback

---

## Security / Privacy Leads

### Role Summary
Security / Privacy Leads coordinate security and privacy considerations so risks are identified, owned, and addressed throughout delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Identify security, privacy, and compliance review needs
- Coordinate threat or privacy assessments and track required mitigations
- Advise the team on security and privacy risks and release considerations
- Escalate unresolved or high-impact risks through the established incident and escalation paths

### Working Relationships
- Work with Developers and QA/Testing to incorporate controls and verify mitigations
- Maintain risk visibility with the PM and advise on escalation and release readiness
- Partner with Stakeholders and Security specialists when review or approval is required

### Typical Communication
- Security and privacy review findings
- Risk-register updates, mitigation tracking, and escalation notices

---

## Data / Analytics Leads

### Role Summary
Data / Analytics Leads help the team measure whether delivered work achieves its intended outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Translate success metrics into instrumentation and measurement plans
- Coordinate event definitions, dashboards, and data-quality checks
- Identify limitations or risks that affect interpretation of results
- Share outcome insights to inform prioritization and iteration

### Working Relationships
- Partner with the PdM to define and evaluate product success metrics
- Coordinate instrumentation needs with Developers and data-quality checks with QA/Testing
- Work with the PM to make delivery and outcome signals visible in project reporting
- Explain relevant findings and caveats to Stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Metric definitions and instrumentation plans
- Dashboards, data-quality findings, and outcome reports

---

## Release / Operations Leads

### Role Summary
Release / Operations Leads coordinate production readiness and operational handoff for a change.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate deployment readiness, monitoring, and post-deployment verification
- Confirm rollback or mitigation plans and operational ownership
- Identify operational dependencies and reliability risks before release
- Support incident triage and follow-up when a release causes issues

### Working Relationships
- Coordinate release timing and readiness tracking with the PM
- Work with Developers and QA/Testing on deployment plans, smoke tests, and verification
- Coordinate monitoring and customer-facing readiness with Support / Customer Enablement Leads
- Escalate operational risks to the PM and relevant technical or security owners

### Typical Communication
- Release checklists and deployment plans
- Monitoring, verification, rollback, and incident updates

---

## Support / Customer Enablement Leads

### Role Summary
Support / Customer Enablement Leads prepare customer-facing teams and materials for a launch and bring customer feedback back to the project team.

### Responsibilities
- Prepare support guidance, FAQs, and enablement materials
- Identify known issues and customer-impacting changes that support teams need to understand
- Gather and route customer feedback and support trends
- Coordinate launch communications and readiness for customer-facing teams

### Working Relationships
- Partner with the PdM to connect customer feedback to product decisions and follow-up work
- Coordinate readiness, timing, and stakeholder communications with the PM
- Work with the Release / Operations Lead on launch timing, known issues, and operational updates
- Share recurring support issues with Developers and QA/Testing for investigation and validation

### Typical Communication
- Support briefs, FAQs, and enablement sessions
- Customer feedback summaries and launch-readiness updates

---

## Assigning Additional Roles

Assign these roles in proportion to project scope and risk. A person may cover more than one role when appropriate, but the team should make ownership and decision-making responsibilities explicit so important work does not fall between roles.
